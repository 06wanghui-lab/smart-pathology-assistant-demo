# Dataset Attribution and Licence

This demonstration includes a non-commercial subset of the Prostate cANcer graDe Assessment (PANDA) Challenge dataset.

Source: [PANDA Challenge dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/prostate-cancer-grade-assessment)

The original PANDA dataset is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

## Changes made

- A subset of five slides was selected for this demonstration.
- The original TIFF whole-slide images were converted into DZI tiles for browser-based viewing.
- Prediction overlays were generated separately and stored as static JSON.

The PANDA-derived slide assets in `public/panda-dzi/` are distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and are not covered by this repository's MIT Licence.
